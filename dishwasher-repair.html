Frequently Asked Questions

Why won't my dishwasher drain?

A dishwasher may not drain because of a clogged filter, blocked drain hose, faulty drain pump, or garbage disposal connection.

Why is my dishwasher leaking?

Leaks are often caused by damaged door gaskets, loose hoses, worn seals, or faulty water inlet valves.

Why are my dishes still dirty?

Dishes may remain dirty due to insufficient water pressure, clogged spray arms, or improper detergent usage.
