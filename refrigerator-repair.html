Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my refrigerator not cooling?

A refrigerator may stop cooling due to dirty condenser coils, a faulty thermostat, compressor problems, or a malfunctioning evaporator fan. Our technicians can diagnose the issue and recommend the right repair.

Why is my refrigerator leaking water?

Water leaks are commonly caused by a clogged defrost drain, damaged water line, or a faulty inlet valve. We can quickly identify and repair the source of the leak.

Do you repair all refrigerator brands?

Yes. We service Whirlpool, GE, Samsung, LG, Frigidaire, KitchenAid, Bosch, Maytag, Kenmore, Amana, and many other major brands.

How long does a refrigerator repair take?

Most refrigerator repairs are completed in a single visit once the problem has been diagnosed and the required parts are available.

Do you offer same-day refrigerator repair?

Yes. We offer same-day service whenever possible throughout Bergen, Passaic, and Essex Counties.
