Frequently Asked Questions

Why won't my washer spin?

A washer may stop spinning because of a worn drive belt, faulty lid switch, damaged motor, or control board issue.

Why is my washing machine leaking water?

WLeaks are often caused by damaged hoses, worn door seals, clogged drains, or faulty water inlet valves.

Why won't my washer drain?

Drain problems are usually caused by clogged drain pumps, blocked hoses, or a defective pump motor.

Do you repair front-load and top-load washers?

Yes. We repair both front-loading and top-loading washing machines from all major brands.

Do you offer same-day washer repair?

Yes. Same-day appointments are available whenever possible in Bergen, Passaic, and Essex Counties.
