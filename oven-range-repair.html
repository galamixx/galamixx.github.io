Frequently Asked Questions

Why won't my oven heat up?

A faulty heating element, igniter, temperature sensor, thermostat, or control board can prevent your oven from heating properly.

Why isn't my gas oven igniting?

Gas ovens commonly fail to ignite because of a worn igniter, gas valve issue, or electronic ignition problem.

Why does my oven cook unevenly?

Uneven cooking usually results from a failing bake element, convection fan, or temperature sensor.

Do you repair gas and electric ovens?

Yes. We repair gas ovens, electric ovens, cooktops, wall ovens, ranges, and stoves.

Do you offer same-day dryer repair?

Yes. We provide same-day appointments whenever possible throughout Bergen, Passaic, and Essex Counties.
